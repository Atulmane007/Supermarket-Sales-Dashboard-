🛒 Supermarket Sales Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The Supermarket Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze supermarket sales performance across different regions, categories, segments, cities, and states. It provides valuable business insights through visualizations, KPIs, and geographic analysis, enabling users to make informed decisions based on sales and profit trends.

🎯 Objectives
Monitor overall sales, profit, and quantity metrics.
Analyze sales performance by region, category, segment, city, and state.
Identify high-performing and low-performing areas.
Track monthly sales trends and profitability.
Support data-driven business decision-making.
